# The friction of transcription is what makes reading stick

Handwriting an extract, filing it, and rewriting it as themes emerge are three
separate passes through the same material, and the material is learned in the
passes rather than in the first reading. Remove the labour and the learning goes
with it: what remains is a stored copy, evidence of having encountered something
rather than of having understood it. The delay before extraction works the same
way from the other direction — weeks on the pile filter out what only seemed
important on the first pass.
