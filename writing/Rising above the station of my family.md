*This is a potentially a draft that leads to a novel for NaNoWriMo*

The story centers around money and the perception of those with it. My parents are almost scared or hold contempt for those who are wealthier. On the other hand I see it as an opportunity to learn and try to be grateful for the fortune of others. *One man is no man.*

How we view those who provide services? i.e. my parents almost revere their doctors and dentists. While they are knowledgeable, that are not irreplaceable, and in the end they work for me.

Life is less of a competition for me than my parents or youngest brother. 

The idea that the pie is finite vs we can make a bigger pie.
