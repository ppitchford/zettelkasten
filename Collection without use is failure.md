# Collection without use is failure

Extracts exist to become something else — a chapter, a talk, a decision — and the
stage that justifies every earlier one is the stage where they are spent. By that
standard a well-kept collection is not a partial success but the same failure as no
collection at all, since the material was gathered and never converted. It also
settles what to do when a store grows faster than the work that would draw on it:
the size of the store is the symptom, and the missing use is the thing to repair.
