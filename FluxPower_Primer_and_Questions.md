# Flux Power Holdings (NASDAQ: FLUX)
## Company Primer and Interview Questions

**Prepared:** August 6, 2026
**Role:** Director of Product, Vista CA, reporting to CEO
**Application sent:** jobs@fluxpower.com
**Note:** All figures below are through fiscal Q3 2026, ended March 31, 2026. The FY2026 10-K is expected around late September 2026 and will supersede much of this.

---

## 1. The Business

Designs, manufactures, and sells lithium-ion battery packs for electrification of industrial and commercial equipment. Primary markets are material handling (forklifts), airport ground support equipment, and stationary energy storage. Products include a proprietary battery management system and telemetry. Headquarters and manufacturing in Vista, CA.

Software line is SkyEMS, a fleet energy management platform. SkyEMS 3.0 shipped June 30, 2026 with AI-driven fleet insights and personalized dashboards. This appears to be the strategic bet.

### Liquidity

- Cash at March 31, 2026 was $372,000, down from $1.3M at June 30, 2025.
- Nine-month operating cash outflow of $5.7M.
- Gibraltar Business Capital revolving facility. $5.7M drawn, up to $10.3M available subject to collateral limits.
- Failed the minimum trailing three-month EBITDA covenant. Event of default. Gibraltar issued a notice of default but continues to allow borrowing while an amendment or waiver is negotiated. Gibraltar retains the ability to restrict access or accelerate repayment.
- Going concern language present in the filing.
- Stockholders' equity improved from a deficit of $(5.4)M at June 30, 2025 to positive $4.6M at March 31, 2026, driven by equity raises and warrant issuances.

### Customer concentration

- Four customers were approximately 90% of Q3 revenue.
- Two customers were approximately 73% of nine-month revenue.
- The largest material handling customer implemented a capital freeze, which management cited as the primary driver of the Q3 revenue decline.

## 5. Reading the Job Description Against This

**Support monetization** is listed as a near-term opportunity. Given the cash position, this is a revenue mandate, not a strategy exercise.

**Team assessment on arrival** is framed as evaluating what you inherit. Operating expenses fell 30% year over year primarily through headcount reduction, so some of the gaps were created by layoffs rather than hiring decisions.

**Product strategy, not roadmap execution** has a practical ceiling while four customers represent 90% of revenue. The real strategic problem is customer diversification, which is a go-to-market problem before it is a product one.

**AI-driven product features and adjacent verticals** align with the CEO's background and the SkyEMS direction. This is the part of the role that plays toward software instincts rather than hardware depth.

**Limited formal authority and earned trust** is stated plainly in the description. Read it literally.

## 6. Interview Questions

Sequencing note: do not open on the balance sheet. Spend the first portion on the role and let them sell. Move to financial risk in the second half. Opening with going-concern language reads as a short seller rather than a candidate.

### Role and team

1. How many people would report to me, and what did the function look like before the headcount reductions?
2. Who owns product today, and why isn't that person being promoted into this?
3. What are the first three decisions you want made in the first 90 days?
4. The description says significant responsibility with limited formal authority. Give me a recent example where product had the call, and one where it didn't.
5. What is the product budget for FY2027, and who approves spend?

### Strategy

6. SkyEMS and software-driven electrification look like the central bet. What share of revenue is software today, and what's the target?
7. Support monetization is called out as a near-term opportunity. Has it been sized, and what's blocking it right now?
8. Where are you willing to stop investing? Name a product line or segment you'd sunset.
9. The FY2023 and FY2024 restatement traced to obsolete inventory from design changes during rapid growth. What changed in the product development process so that doesn't recur?

Question 9 is the highest-value question on this list. It demonstrates the filings were read and goes directly at the intersection of product decisions and financial control.

### Demand and concentration

10. Four customers were about 90% of Q3 revenue. Has the largest customer's capital freeze lifted, and what does order visibility look like now?
11. What is the concentration target twelve months out, and which accounts get you there?
12. How much of the OEM white-label engagement has converted to committed volume versus pricing conversations?
13. How exposed is the bill of materials to tariffs, and who absorbs the cost?

### Runway

14. The Q3 filing disclosed a covenant default on the Gibraltar facility. Has a waiver or amendment been executed, and what are the revised terms?
15. How much of the $40M equity facility has been drawn, and at what average price?
16. What's the current cash position and expected burn through the end of the fiscal year?
17. The Nasdaq bid price compliance window runs to roughly late January. Is a reverse split under consideration?
18. What will the FY2026 10-K show that the Q3 numbers didn't?

### If it progresses to an offer

19. Base, bonus structure, and how equity is being handled at the current share price.
20. Severance terms and any change-of-control provision.

Question 20 is the one most candidates skip. Given the balance sheet, it may be the most important term in the package.

## 7. My Position

**Strong match:** Function-building from scratch. Player-coach operating model. Influence without formal authority. Strategy ownership with a defensible no-go decision. Executive interface. Local to Vista.

**Real gaps:** No hardware or manufacturing product experience. No ISO or quality systems ownership. No engineering degree. No industry network in material handling.

**Nearest adjacencies:** Self-service kiosk hardware deployed across live venues (Legends Global). Enterprise partner integrations with Amazon, Walmart, and Banfield, which resemble Flux Power's end-customer profile.
