# Plain vanilla becomes cumbersome as websites grow and add dynamic elements

Plain vanilla does not provide the structure or tools to help maintain consistency. As a website grows, managing multiple files or handling complex interactions becomes cumbersome and time-consuming. If the website doesn't require complex features like user authentication, state management, or dynamic content rendering, plain vanilla is more than enough. For instance, a small business website, portfolio, or informational page.
