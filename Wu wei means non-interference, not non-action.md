# Wu wei means non-interference, not non-action
The correct translation of “wu wei” is “non-interference”. Most western translations make the mistakes of translating it as “non-action”.

The *Daodejing* is a treatise on how to run a system – a state, society, government, or business. 

Wu wei is not the idea of doing nothing, but of not doing too much or not forcing things.
