# Pre-commit your stopping conditions before the pressure arrives

Deciding in advance the specific conditions under which you will stop removes
feeling from the equation at the moment it is least trustworthy.

- [[Insecurity work produces no progress but repeats without notice]] — Insecurity work has no stopping condition, which is why it repeats unnoticed; the pre-commitment is the defense against it.
- [[The antidote to outcome-driven anxiety is treating the process as the destination]] — Both remove the moment's feeling from a decision that feeling would distort.
