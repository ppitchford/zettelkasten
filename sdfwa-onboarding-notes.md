# SDFWA Member Shop — Onboarding Video Notes

Source: two-part orientation video. Gary Anderson (SDFWA president, member shop founding member) on history and expectations; Nancy Bosker (admissions director) on provisional program mechanics.

---

## Organization

- SDFWA founded ~42 years ago to run an annual woodworking exhibition at what was then the Del Mar Fair — the **Design in Wood** show.
- That show was the association's sole purpose for its first 30 years.
- Structure: SDFWA is the umbrella, with two pillars beneath it — Design in Wood, and the member shop.
- Everyone is an SDFWA member. Tiers:
  - **Bronze** — $35, association only
  - **Silver / Gold** — association plus shop privileges

## Member shop origin

- Conceived ~10 years ago in response to declining membership: loss of high school and junior college woodworking programs, closure of military hobby shops, and an aging, homogeneous member base.
- Three years spent on the business plan and fundraising.
- Opened in Miramar eight years ago — 3,000 sq ft, 125 members.
- Now past a second major renovation, membership capped at 400.
- Stated guiding principle: build a woodworking *community*, not just a shop.

## Recurring programs

| Program | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Eye Opener** | Last Saturday morning monthly | Doors 8:00 a.m.; social hour until 9:00, then general meeting plus a demo or presentation |
| **SIGs** (special interest groups) | Varies | Carving, digital tools (laser / 3D printer / CNC), luthier, hand tool, epoxy, others |
| **Holiday Gift Sale** | Friday + Saturday before Thanksgiving | Association's sole fundraiser; >$60,000 in member-made goods sold last year |
| **Gift builds** | Friday mornings weekly + first Saturday monthly | Own designs or group projects |

Eye Opener extras: a local tree recycler sells milled lumber at discount; a member photographer shoots completed projects free of charge.

## Volunteer expectation

- All-volunteer operation. No paid employees.
- Joining is framed as a commitment to contribute. Anderson's phrasing: if you're unwilling, don't sign up — at 90 days they'll shake your hand and wish you well.
- The stated payoff is a long-term role matched to your interests and skill set.

## Provisional membership — 3 months

**Gate:** attend orientation, pass the online safety test, receive certification and insurance coverage for shop use.

**Six tasks to complete:**

- [ ] Attend an Eye Opener
- [ ] Attend a holiday gift build
- [ ] Use a shop slot
- [ ] Attend a special interest group
- [ ] Six volunteer hours — minimum two hours each across three separate tasks
- [ ] Establish long-term volunteer assignment

Tasks are tracked on a signature sheet issued at orientation; collect a signature at each event. Submit the completed sheet and the term converts from three months to a full year.

The long-term assignment is the one flagged as most important. Arranged with shop leadership near the end of the provisional term, after you've had time to observe where the gaps are.

## Shop membership and logistics

- **Silver** — 15 slots / $295
- **Gold** — 50 slots / $550
- Extra slots — five for $50, purchasable throughout the year
- A **slot** is a three-hour reserved block, booked online. Up to six members per slot plus a shift supervisor who monitors safety and advises on request.
- Unused slots roll over at renewal; tier can be switched at renewal.
- Email is the primary communication channel.
- A cohort contact emails monthly during the provisional term for status — responses expected.
- Orientation date is the critical calendar item: safety test review, certification, shop access.
