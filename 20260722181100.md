# Santa Maria seasoning

A dry seasoning blend for Santa Maria-style tri-tip.

## Ingredients
- 1¼ tsp salt
- 1¼ tsp garlic powder
- 1¼ tsp onion powder
- ¾ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp dried rosemary
- ¼ tsp Aleppo pepper flakes
- ⅛ tsp cayenne

## Substitution
- For the Aleppo pepper flakes, substitute smoked paprika and sugar.
